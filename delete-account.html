Delete your account

Operator: Rik Kleine · Contact: rik.kleine@gmail.com
Last updated: 1 November 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap Delete account
  3. Confirm Delete permanently

That removes your Cognito sign-in user, cloud backups in AWS, telemetry and AI credit / quota rows for your account id, and training data on this device. Unused workout credits on the deleted account are forfeited. Play purchase history and refunds remain with Google. Data on other phones is your responsibility to clear.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email rik.kleine@gmail.com from the address on the account. We will delete the account after we can confirm it is yours.

Local-only use (no account) has no cloud account to delete. Uninstalling the app removes local data unless Android backup kept a copy.
