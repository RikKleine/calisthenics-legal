Privacy Policy

Controller: Rik Kleine · Contact: rik.kleine@gmail.com
Last updated: 30 August 2026

This policy describes how Calisthenics Coach (“the app”) processes personal data. It is written for a Netherlands-based operator under the GDPR (AVG). It is a working draft for store submission — have a lawyer review it before you rely on it commercially.

1. What the app does

Calisthenics Coach is a training app for planning and logging calisthenics workouts. You can use it locally on your device. An optional account enables cloud backup, sync, and AI features (workout generation, skill assessment, session check-in).

2. Data we process

On your device (always)

  • Workout history, skill levels, baselines, athlete notes, equipment and theme preferences
  • Optional local backup files you export (for example to Downloads)

This stays on the device unless you sign in and sync, or you export a backup yourself.

If you create an account

  • Email address and a password stored by Amazon Cognito (we do not see your password)
  • A unique account id (user_sub)
  • If you use Google Sign-In: email and name/profile as provided by Google, used only to create or sign in to your account

If you use cloud backup / sync

A copy of your training export (workouts, skill levels, baselines, notes, AI usage summary) in Amazon S3, stored under your account id in AWS region eu-west-1 (Ireland).

If you use AI features

  • The prompt we send to the model (selected skills, levels, notes, recent workout context, equipment, session style)
  • Token usage needed to run and limit the service
  • Optional quality signals: generation feedback, suggested exercises, illustration reports

AI requests go through our backend in AWS to OpenAI. Do not put names, health diagnoses, or other sensitive information in athlete notes.

Telemetry (when cloud is enabled)

  • Event type, time, and a small payload (for example AI usage, a suggested exercise name)
  • Stored in Amazon DynamoDB (eu-west-1), keyed by your account id, retained about 1 year

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Training logs can look like fitness data. We treat them as regular personal data about how you train, not as medical records. The app is not a medical device.

4. Who else sees data (processors)

  • Amazon Web Services (Ireland / eu-west-1): accounts, backups, jobs, telemetry
  • OpenAI: content of AI requests and model output. OpenAI is a processor/sub-processor outside the EEA; transfers rely on their current GDPR terms and Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Google (only if you tap Continue with Google): authentication

5. How long we keep data

  • Local data: until you clear the device or uninstall (Android backup settings may retain app data)
  • Cloud backups: until you delete them by deleting your account, or we close the service
  • AI jobs: about 48 hours
  • Telemetry: about 1 year
  • Account (Cognito): until you delete the account

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and object to legitimate-interest processing. You can:

  • Export a backup from the app
  • Delete the account in Settings → Delete account (removes cloud backups, telemetry, quota rows, and the Cognito user, and clears training data on that device)
  • Email rik.kleine@gmail.com

You may complain to the Dutch DPA (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

7. Children

The app is intended for people 16 or older. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 16.

8. Security

Access to cloud AI and backups requires a signed-in account. Backups are stored per account id. Release builds do not embed an OpenAI API key. No method is perfectly secure.

9. Android permissions

We only request what the feature needs (network, notifications/timer, optional storage for your own backup files). Details are in the in-app Permissions note.

10. Changes

We will update the “Last updated” date when this policy changes. Material changes will be pointed out in the app or by email if you have an account.
